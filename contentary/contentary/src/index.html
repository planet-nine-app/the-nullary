How to Use Contentary

  1. Create SVG Slides - Design your vertical slides (1080x1920) in your favorite tool
  2. Import SVGs - Click the "+ Add Slide" button in the timeline below
  3. Enable Camera - Click the 📷 button to show your picture-in-picture
  4. Record - Click ⏺️ to start/stop recording your presentation
  5. Export - Save your recordings and slide data

SVG Requirements

  • Dimensions: 1080x1920 (9:16 aspect ratio)
  • Format: SVG files only
  • Vertical orientation (portrait mode)
  • Clean, scalable graphics work best
💡 Pro Tip: Design your slides in tools like Figma, Adobe Illustrator, or Inkscape, then export as SVG. This app focuses on presenting your slides with camera overlay - perfect for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts!
